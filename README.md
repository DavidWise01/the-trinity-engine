# THE TRINITY ENGINE · the cross, on the fly

> A sphere of **UD0** — the ROOT0 universe / biosphere. Domain: **PROSOCHĒ**.

THE TRINITY ENGINE — ROOT0's Trinity cross made a LIVE, on-the-fly-editable attention engine. The cross maps each score through the Cayley transform z=(1+iωd)/(1−iωd)·e^(d/T) onto the unit circle (|z|=1, exact), weights each token by how much its phase aligns with the resultant R=Σz, and decomposes the mass into three cardinals — forward (in-phase) + two shadows (the rejected anti-phase). ⚑ GREEN (node-verified): the kernel is exact and recomputed live; at ω=0 it collapses to softmax EXACTLY (max diff 1e−16) — so the Trinity cross is a GENERALIZATION of softmax with a phase axis, not a replacement. Drag ω to morph softmax → Trinity and watch the weights, the three cardinals, and the complex-plane geometry (z on the circle, the resultant R, the sector wedges) respond; every knob (ω, T, centre-seed, phases, input) rewrites the engine, and you can drag the input bars to reshape what it reads. AMBER: the forward/shadow names are a figure over an exact decomposition; the input is a toy score vector, not a trained model's Q·K. RED: trained native it TIES softmax within ~1.2% ([[trained-on-the-cross]]) — competitive, not superior, ~1.75× compute; and the engine reveals the honest catch — for clustered inputs the mass is ~all forward, the shadows only fill under genuine phase diversity, which is why the extra axis may not pay on real data.
